# UpCode Academy

> UpCode Academy was a Singapore-based coding school offering short bootcamps and part-time courses (SQL, Python, Web Development with Ruby on Rails, Data Science, Computer Vision, IoT) taught by industry practitioners. It was operated by 40 Tasks (a 500 Global portfolio company whose earlier product was the LOCO location-based search app). The company appears defunct: upcodeacademy.com serves a Cloudflare 522 dead-origin error (and was a bare default server page by December 2025), and 40tasks.com no longer resolves in DNS.
